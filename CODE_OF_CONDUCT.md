# Code of Conduct

Harness Pet is a friendly project, and we want everyone to feel welcome here, whatever their experience, background or identity.

**Please do:**

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to open source.
- Assume good intent, and give feedback about the work, not the person.
- Accept that the maintainer may say no to an idea. It's never personal.

**Not acceptable:**

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexual, violent or hateful language or imagery.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Repeatedly ignoring a request to stop.

**If something goes wrong:** email the maintainer privately at `0vknzqgws@mozmail.com`. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block people who don't follow this code.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
